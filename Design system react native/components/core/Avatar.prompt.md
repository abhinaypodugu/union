Person or committee avatar. Initials on a soft, deterministic tint per name; `official` adds the blue seal used on official announcements.

```jsx
<Avatar name="Lakshmi Rao" size={40} official />
<AvatarStack names={['A B', 'C D', 'E F', 'G H', 'I J']} />
```
- Sizes: 24 (dense lists), 32 (comments), 40 (posts), 56+ (profile).
